# Provenance — starter decklists

Written by hand. The upstream dataset (`../PROVENANCE.md`) ships the product
*catalogue* — the 17 card types in each starter — but never the quantities, and
a decklist is quantities: 1 Leader plus 50 cards with multiplicities.

## Sources

The official product page states the shape of the box but not the list:
"Constructed Deck x 1 (51 cards)", "Leader Card x 1", "Super Rare x 2",
"Common x 14" — 17 types.
<https://en.onepiece-cardgame.com/products/decks/st01-04.php>

Per-card quantities were transcribed from:

- <https://tier-one-onepiece.jp/blog/st01-cardlists/> (ST-01)
- <https://tier-one-onepiece.jp/blog/st02-cardlists/> (ST-02)
- <https://onepieceplayer.com/set/st-02-starter-deck-worst-generation/>
  (independent second reading of ST-02; it agrees card for card)

Both lists were accepted only after they added up: 9 cards at 4 copies and 7 at
2 is the single split of 17 types that reaches exactly 50, and the two SR cards
of each deck sit among the twos. Transcriptions that did not total 50 were
discarded rather than adjusted.

ST-01 is the simpler shape — `ST01-002`…`ST01-010` at 4, `ST01-011`…`ST01-017`
at 2 — and only one source was found for it, so it is the weaker of the two
transcriptions. If a copy of the physical deck is ever at hand, check ST-01
first.
